About

Intelligent self-evolving agents for software engineering.

I am a researcher affiliated with Siemens AG and the Technical University of Munich. My work investigates how LLMs and multi-agent systems can support complex software development.

We are moving beyond single model interactions to self-evolving agents with specialized roles, shared representations, and coordinated workflows.

Research Focus

Intelligent systems for engineering work

01

Agentic Software Engineering

Studying how specialized LLM agents can coordinate across requirements, architecture, implementation, analysis, testing, and debugging.

02

Multi-Agent Coordination

Designing shared representations, communication protocols, and orchestration strategies for coherent work across agent teams.

03

AI for Software Transformation

Applying generative systems to turn exploratory artifacts into maintainable, production-oriented software while preserving behavior.
