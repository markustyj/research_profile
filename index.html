01 / About

Building useful AI for the way software is actually made.

I am affiliated with Siemens AG and the Technical University of Munich. My work centers on practical questions around intelligent software systems: how agents reason together, how language models support engineering tasks, and how these systems can be evaluated in realistic settings.

I am developing my research agenda at the meeting point of industrial practice and academic inquiry. I value careful experiments, reproducible engineering, and tools that provide clear benefits to their users.
