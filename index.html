Large Language Models Self-Evolving Agents Software Engineering

I am a Ph.D. researcher affiliated with Siemens AG and the Technical University of Munich.

My work explores how LLMs, intelligent agents can support complex software development.
The research focus is moving beyond single language models to self-evolving agents for industrial software.
